# SpeciaList Validation MVP usability plan

## Objective

Validate whether Clients, qualified Experts, and Academy Candidates understand the product, trust its verification model, and can complete their primary journey without facilitation.

## Recommended sample

- 5–7 potential Clients
- 5–7 professionals from at least one initial category
- 4–5 people interested in the Academy pathway

Prioritise behavioral observations over broad preference questions.

## Session format

1. Explain that the product is a prototype, not the participant.
2. Ask the participant to think aloud.
3. Read the scenario without explaining how to complete it.
4. Note hesitation, misinterpretation, backtracking, and assistance required.
5. Ask follow-up questions only after the task.
6. Export the prototype’s locally captured feedback at the end.

## Client scenario

> You need a vetted nanny for two children in Clapham, starting in three weeks. Find suitable expert help and complete the consultation.

Observe:

- Correct role selection
- Understanding of natural-language intake
- Ability to review what will be shared
- Understanding of £25 authorisation and capture
- Trust in the matched Expert
- Ability to find the outcome and follow-up

Ask:

- What did you think SpeciaList would do after you submitted the request?
- When did you believe you would be charged?
- What made the Expert feel trustworthy or untrustworthy?
- What would you do next after reading the outcome?

## Expert scenario

> You are an experienced nanny with current DBS and paediatric first-aid evidence. Apply to join, then handle a matching Client request.

Observe:

- Verification expectations
- Evidence-checklist comprehension
- Profile-writing difficulty
- Understanding that submission is not approval
- Confidence accepting or declining the request
- Understanding of the £17.50 payout

Ask:

- Which requirement, if any, felt unreasonable?
- Did you have enough information to accept the request?
- Was the relationship between Client price and Expert payout clear?
- What would stop you from joining?

## Academy scenario

> You want to provide career-application support but lack formal credentials. Explore the pathway and complete the sample assessment.

Observe:

- Difference between Candidate and verified professional status
- Understanding of Academy Expert status and its limits
- Perceived assessment quality
- Response to supervised consultations
- Interest in continuing

Ask:

- What would Academy Expert status allow you to do?
- What would it not qualify you to do?
- What additional evidence would make the pathway credible?
- Would you complete three supervised consultations?

## Measures

Track:

- Task completion without help
- Time on task
- Critical errors
- Number of facilitator interventions
- Pricing comprehension
- Verification comprehension
- Confidence before and after the journey

## Decision thresholds

- At least 80% identify the correct role without help.
- At least 80% complete the Client request unaided.
- At least 70% correctly explain payment timing.
- At least 75% identify what was verified about the Expert.
- At least 75% of Experts understand application versus approval.
- At least 75% understand Academy Expert status does not replace regulated qualifications.

## Evidence handling

The prototype stores milestone feedback in the participant’s browser only. Use the Testing Guide to download the JSON after each session, then clear captured feedback before handing the device to another participant.
